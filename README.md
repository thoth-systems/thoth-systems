## Research vision

THOTH is a research and engineering initiative developing a model-independent cognitive architecture designed to support autonomous reasoning, independent judgment, experience-driven behavioral adaptation, and durable intellectual continuity across interactions.

The objective is to move beyond isolated language-model interactions toward a coherent, long-term intellectual partnership without requiring the user to continuously manage the system's memory or behavioral consistency.

## Engineering approach

Two architectural candidates are planned for comparative evaluation:

- **Application Orchestration** — behavioral coordination at the application layer.
- **Independent Behavioral Runtime** — a dedicated runtime for behavioral policy and continuity.

Both approaches will be evaluated against shared engineering and behavioral criteria. A hybrid architecture may be considered based on empirical results.
